# myfintrack

A comprehensive personal finance tracking and planning application that helps you manage your finances like a Certified Financial Planner (CFP®).

## Features

- **Account Linking via Plaid**: Securely connect your bank and investment accounts to automatically sync transactions and account balances
- **Statement Upload**: Locally upload financial statements or link to a folder for automatic statement processing
- **Financial Planning**: Get comprehensive financial planning insights and recommendations similar to those provided by professional financial planners
- **Transaction Tracking**: Automatically categorize and track all your transactions
- **Net Worth Analysis**: Monitor your overall financial health and net worth over time
- **Budget Planning**: Create budgets and track spending against your financial goals
